    Ordering guide

    How to order your usual meals so the numbers stay close to what you log.

    • Ask for “less oil, no extra butter” every time. Restaurant gravies can hide 200–300 kcal.
    • Dry tikka, dal, rajma and chole beat makhani, malai and kofta. Phulka beats naan or paratha.
    • Not sure about a restaurant dish? Log it 15–20% higher than your guess.

    Late-night cravings

    • Your snack is planned, not a slip. Keep 150–250 kcal free for 23:30–00:30, since you’re awake until 2 am.
    • Stock whey, Greek yogurt, roasted chana and makhana, so ordering isn’t the easy option.
    • Eat dinner by 21:00–21:30 with a big protein portion. It’s the strongest craving blocker.
    • After the snack, brush your teeth. That’s your “kitchen closed” signal.

    Workout plan: 4 days, 60 minutes, upper/lower

    Run the sessions in order, not on fixed weekdays: Upper A, Lower A, Upper B, Lower B, then repeat. Miss a day and you just do the next one. The Workout tab always shows which session is next.

    • Each session: 5 minutes of warm-up, about 45 minutes of lifting with 1.5–2.5 minutes rest, then 10 minutes of incline walking if time allows.
    • Weeks 1–2 (returning): 2 sets of each exercise, stopping when you could still do 3 more reps.
    • From week 3: full sets, stopping 1–2 reps short of failure. Hit the top of the rep range on every set? Add 2.5 kg (upper body) or 5 kg (lower body).
    • Every 8th week, cut sets in half for a lighter week.

    Habits that make it stick

    • Sleep. Six hours raises hunger the next day. Move bedtime 15 minutes earlier each week until you reach 00:30–7:30.
    • Steps. Your everyday 7,000 is counted automatically. A 30-minute brisk walk adds about 3,300 steps, enough to close the Steps ring.
    • Motivation. Chasing rings beats chasing a number on the scale. Aim to close Move and Protein every day; Fuel on most days.
    • Off days. Log the bad days too. Honest data is what makes the yearly picture useful.
    • Check-in. Weigh in 2–3 mornings a week. If the 2-week average isn’t moving, raise the planned deficit by 100 kcal on the Profile tab.
